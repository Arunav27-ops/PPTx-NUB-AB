Conversion Examples

Infix → Prefix: (2 + 8) * 5 → * + 2 8 5
Infix → Postfix: (2 + 8) * 5 → 2 8 + 5 *
Postfix Evaluation: 5 3 16 * 2 3 ^ / + → 5 + (3*16)/(2^3) = 11
Shunting Yard: Used to convert infix to postfix using a stack and a queue.

Quick Reference

  • Left-associative: evaluate left to right (+, -, *, /)
  • Right-associative: evaluate right to left (^)
  • BODMAS – Bracket, Of, Division, Multiplication, Addition, Subtraction
  • PEDMAS – Parenthesis, Exponent, Division, Multiplication, Addition, Subtraction
